# E1 claim ledger

| Claim | Evidence | Permitted wording | Prohibited extension |
|---|---|---|---|
| Random orthogonal maps impair accepted BuNN prediction | -8.86 balanced-accuracy points; 95% CI -12.36 to -4.78; Holm-adjusted exact p=0.0038 | The trained predictions depended on properties of the learned map system. | The maps recovered biological bundle geometry. |
| Identity maps impair prediction descriptively | -4.72 points; 95% CI -8.56 to -0.29; Holm p=0.1404 | Removing learned orientation was associated with worse prediction, but the multiplicity-controlled test did not reject. | Identity maps were conclusively inferior under confirmatory multiplicity control. |
| Exact node-map placement has weaker support | Shuffle effect -1.94 points; 95% CI -3.77 to 0.05; Holm p=0.1404 | Preserving the learned map collection while shuffling nodes retained more performance than random replacement. | Node identity is irrelevant. |
| Constructed topology has a modest effect | Rewiring effect -1.23 points; 95% CI -2.49 to -0.05; Holm p=0.1404; flip fraction 6.96% | Predictions showed limited dependence on the selected positive-edge topology. | The graph is anatomically valid or biologically causal. |
| Representation and prediction changes are weakly associated | Absolute descriptive Spearman rho no larger than 0.21 across 72 cells | Diagnostic and predictive changes did not closely co-vary in this audit. | Representation change cannot mediate prediction in general. |
| E1 explains but does not replace Study 1 | E1 used 360 accepted checkpoints after Study 1 was frozen | BuNN used its maps internally but still showed no Study 1 advantage over GCN or elastic net. | E1 rescues, overturns, or independently confirms Study 1. |

All E1 claims are conditional on the accepted ABIDE-I cohort, preprocessing, AAL-116 representation, positive-edge density grid, trained checkpoints, and intervention definitions. E1 is post-hoc mechanistic evidence.
