The experiment isolates the propagation operator while holding the surrounding
pipeline fixed (\cref{fig:study-design}). We completed and audited the
classical baselines before fitting the neural models. Five propagation or
capacity-control variants then shared one neural backbone. They used identical
outer sites, grouped inner folds, candidate budgets, stopping rules, and final
seeds. Before examining performance, we verified the complete neural artifact
and froze the confirmatory analysis code and decision rules.

\subsection{Dataset and technical quality control}

We used the ABIDE-I C-PAC derivatives released by the Preprocessed Connectomes
Project \citep{dimartino2014abide,abidepcp}. The source was the
band-pass-filtered AAL ROI time-series derivative without global-signal
regression.
The AAL atlas defines macroscopic anatomical regions in standardized space
\citep{tzourio2002aal}. Eligibility required a valid header, finite numeric
values, non-zero variance in every ROI, and a finite resulting connectome. Of
\StudyParentRows{} downloaded files, \StudyTechnicalExclusions{} failed the
zero-variance check. The retained cohort contained
\StudyParticipants{} participants (\StudyASD{} ASD and \StudyControls{}
controls) from \StudySites{} evaluable sites. These checks establish technical
eligibility, not population representativeness.

\subsection{Connectomes, node features, and graphs}

For each participant, we calculated Pearson correlations between every pair of
\StudyROIs{} ROI time series and applied Fisher's $r$-to-$z$ transformation.
We set the diagonal to zero. The \StudyEdges{} values in the lower triangle of
the finite, symmetric matrix formed the classical feature vector. For the
neural models, each node received its complete connectivity row as a
\StudyROIs{}-dimensional feature. The node inputs were thus globally informed
before any message passing occurred.

At nonzero densities, we retained the strongest positive off-diagonal
associations symmetrically at 1, 5, 10, and 20 percent. Density zero contained
only the matched identity or learned-local anchor. The exclusion of negative
associations was a pre-specified computational choice, not a biological
interpretation.

\subsection{Classical baselines}

We fit three logistic baselines: covariates alone, vectorized connectomes with
elastic-net regularization, and connectomes combined with covariates. Elastic-net
regularization combines $\ell_1$ and $\ell_2$ penalties and is appropriate for
correlated high-dimensional predictors \citep{zou2005elastic}. Covariates were
age, sex, mean framewise displacement, and usable scan length. Scaling,
feature processing, and tuning used training sites only.

\subsection{Shared neural backbone and operators}

All variants shared the encoder, hidden width, depth, nonlinearities,
normalization, dropout, pooling, classifier, optimizer, training schedule, and
tuning budget. Only propagation and the capacity associated with learned maps
changed:

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Identity}: no inter-node propagation.
  \item \textbf{Learned-local}: the BuNN map generator and node-wise updates,
  but no inter-node diffusion, controlling for additional learned capacity.
  \item \textbf{GCN}: normalized neighborhood aggregation
  \citep{kipf2017gcn}.
  \item \textbf{Trivial bundle}: the same diffusion construction with identity
  transport maps.
  \item \textbf{Learned BuNN}: learned orthogonal node maps followed by bundle
  diffusion \citep{bamberger2025bundle}.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Nested held-out-site evaluation}

Each of the \StudySites{} sites served once as the outer test site. Four
grouped folds within the remaining sites selected among four equal-budget
AdamW candidates using two tuning seeds. We refit the selected configuration
with five fixed final seeds. Test-site data did not enter scaling, tuning,
early stopping, or configuration selection. Equal-site mean balanced accuracy
was the primary predictive endpoint. Pooled balanced accuracy and AUROC were
secondary.

\subsection{Density curves and representation diagnostics}

The primary predictive estimand was the normalized trapezoidal area under the
balanced-accuracy curve across the full density grid. GCN and trivial-bundle
curves used identity at density zero. The learned-BuNN curve used the
parameter-matched learned-local anchor. Evaluating the entire curve prevented
the analysis from selecting a favorable density after the fact.

After every propagation layer, we mapped embeddings into a common learned
frame before comparing nodes. Normalized effective rank at layer 2 was the
primary representation endpoint. Secondary measures were normalized
dispersion, mean pairwise cosine similarity, invariant edge-transport
distance, and the change in rank from encoder to layer 2. Prediction and
representation were analyzed as co-occurring outcomes, without a mediation or
causal claim.

\subsection{Statistical analysis and decision rule}

We averaged the five final seeds within each site and configuration before
site-level inference. Paired percentile intervals came from 10,000 bootstrap
resamples of the \StudySites{} held-out sites. Curve contrasts also received
exact two-sided paired sign-flip tests. Density-specific tests were secondary
and Holm-corrected within each named contrast family.

The positive-result rule required three conditions: stronger representation
preservation than GCN, better held-out prediction than GCN and the
no-propagation controls, and performance above the strongest regularized
baseline. We reported a confidence interval containing zero as no detected
advantage. We ruled out a practically meaningful gain only when the upper
bound excluded the pre-specified +\PracticalMargin{} balanced-accuracy margin.

\subsection{Run integrity and reproducibility}

Long runs wrote atomic checkpoints after each site and recorded immutable
hashes for the data, configuration, and source. Deterministic site ownership,
resume validation, and independent completion audits guarded the distributed
run. The accepted neural artifact covered every outer site and passed checks
for coverage, recomputation, diagnostics, runtime, and warnings before
unblinding. Manuscript values are generated from a hash-bound result snapshot
instead of being copied by hand.
