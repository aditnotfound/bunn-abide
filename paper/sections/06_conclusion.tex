\section{Conclusion}

Under the pre-specified ABIDE-I pipeline, learned orthogonal bundle transport
did not produce a more favorable held-out-site performance-density curve than
ordinary GCN aggregation, did not surpass identity or learned-local controls,
and remained below a regularized connectome baseline. Gauge-aware matched-
anchor diagnostics likewise did not support the proposed representation-
preservation advantage. The evidence therefore supports a narrow conclusion:
bundle transport changed the operator and increased capacity and runtime, but
no predictive benefit was detected in this dense, globally informed
functional-connectome setting.
